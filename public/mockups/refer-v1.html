A referral marketplace with zero referrers in it

Measured against production before drawing anything: company_referrers = 0, open = 0, company_referral_requests = 0, against 95 accounts that could publish a desk. So every search on this page returns "No open referrers for that company yet" — today that is not an edge case, it is the only reachable result. The shipped page makes search the hero and puts that sentence in muted grey underneath. The fix is to lead with the side that works.

What changed, and why each one

  1. ⚠️ The empty result is the DEFAULT, so it gets a designed state. With 0 referrers, 100% of searches land there. The shipped page says “No open referrers for that company yet.” in muted grey with nothing to do next — a dead end on the only path the page can take. It now offers the two things that actually move: be told when supply appears, or become the supply.
  2. Two lanes, and the employee lane is primary. A marketplace with no sellers has nothing to sell. The ring marks it; the other lane is not hidden, because a candidate with the link still arrives here.
  3. The headline is the product, not the instruction. “Ask an employee for a referral” tells a visitor what to do on a page where doing it fails. The new line says what the thing is and sets up the three-step mechanism below.
  4. The mechanism IS the reassurance. “They decide” is the honest frame and also the credible one: nobody fills in a form for a stranger without knowing who sees it. All three steps are true of the shipped code.
  5. “Work email never stored” is verified, not assumed. The HLD states work email is deliberately not a column, because a public SELECT policy would leak it.

⚠️ Two claims are deliberately absent. No count of referrers or companies — it is zero, and a counter that reads 0 is worse than silence. No “X referrals made” either. Both go in the moment the numbers exist and clear a floor, which is the rule /api/score-standing already follows.

What changed here

  1. The headline names the person and what they can refer for, which is the only thing on the page that makes the form worth filling. The shipped version leads with the eyebrow “EMPLOYEE REFERRAL” and a generic “Ask for a referral at {company}” buried as the third line of a card.
  2. Five unlabelled placeholders become labelled fields. Placeholder-as-label disappears the moment you type, so you cannot check your own form before sending — and two of these fields are the ones she judges you on.
  3. The two hints are the valuable part and they are honest: she opens the link, and she reads the pitch first. Neither is a stat.
  4. “She decides” appears twice, deliberately — once as the lede and once as step 2 — because a candidate's unspoken question on this page is whether this is an application or a favour. It is a favour.
  5. Name + company in the title tag, so a shared link previews as “Priya can refer at Stripe” rather than the generic page title.

⚠️ “Replies in ~2 days” is a PLACEHOLDER and must not ship as drawn. There is no response-time data — 0 requests exist — so it is either computed from real medians once there are enough of them, or it is cut. Drawn here only to show where such a chip would sit. Everything else on both states is backed by shipped code or by the HLD.
