What's wrong

  1. A door onto another door. The button opens the SignInDialog modal on top of this page. Two sign-in surfaces, one after the other, for one decision.
  2. It asks while showing nothing. The visitor clicked Interview Prep for a reason. The page knows the destination and does nothing with it — no preview, no sample, no reason to want it.
  3. It leads with our requirement, not their gain. "needs a free account" is our constraint. What they get is buried in a subordinate clause.
  4. The exit competes with the entrance. "Back to home" sits directly under the CTA, the second-most prominent thing on a page whose only job is conversion.
  5. Contrast bug. color:#fff on var(--accent) measures 2.53:1 in dark mode — the exact pairing DESIGN.md invariant 7 forbids. Should be --on-fill.
  6. A 420px card in a 100dvh field. Most of the screen is empty and the card carries no weight.

Direction A — recommended

Show the thing, ask once

The form is on the page, so the modal is gone and there is one surface for one decision. The right half shows the destination half-open with real content, faded rather than locked — it says there is more behind this without claiming a number we cannot source.
