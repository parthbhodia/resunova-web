Account menu

Three directions against what ships today, each drawn at the real token values and the real icon set. Light and dark side by side, on the page background they actually open over, because half of “more visible” is separation from what is behind them.

What is wrong with the current one

  1. It never says whose account it is. No name, no email, no plan. The avatar shows one letter. If you have a personal and a university account this menu cannot tell you which one you are in, and that is the one job an account menu has that nothing else does.
  2. No icons, while the rail directly above it is all icons. This is the only nav surface in the shell rendering bare text, and the glyphs already exist in nav-icons.tsx. Nothing new has to be drawn.
  3. Contact, Terms and Privacy take three full rows at the same weight as My Résumés. That is a quarter of the menu spent on links almost nobody opens.
  4. Dark mode is an action dressed as a destination. Every row around it navigates; that one flips a setting and stays put, and looks identical.
  5. It sits on the page rather than above it — white panel, near-white page, soft shadow. Stronger elevation and a real border are most of “more visible”.
  6. “© 2026 Resunova” is a line of chrome in a menu you opened to go somewhere.
