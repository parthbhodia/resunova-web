The nav becomes a rail: the funnel down the left edge, and Templates stops hiding

Structure borrowed from the reference, then designed for us: the order is the product's own funnel (score it, tailor it, pick a template, keep your résumés, apply), every control has a visible label at rest, the upgrade is a labeled action instead of an anonymous crown, and the scan quota rides the rail so a metered user always knows where they stand. Two clicks become one everywhere, and the item users can't find today is promoted.

What was designed, not copied

Tailor

Active is a filled pill, label inside. Ink on the theme's text color, both themes; the label never disappears, so no tooltip hunting and every item keeps an accessible name.

Templates

Templates is promoted to the rail and opens the template gallery directly, one click from anywhere. The Resume Builder drawer dies; Tailor and Templates are both top-level.

The quota lives above Upgrade, so the count and the fix for it sit together. Metered accounts only, counting down at rest rather than appearing at zero.

Upgrade

The upgrade says what it is. The reference's bare crown has no label and no accessible name; ours is a labeled action.

Grouping is the funnel: work on the document (Analyze, Tailor, Templates, Resumes) · use it (Jobs, Prep) · meta (Feedback, plan, account). Cover letter, History, theme, Contact and the Advisor view (when allowed) move into the account menu and the surfaces that own them; nothing is deleted. Mobile keeps the bottom tab bar.
