# Distribution notes: h1b-visa-sponsorship-job-postings

Draft for human review. Nothing here has been posted anywhere.

## Headline stat

95.7% of 102,857 active US postings (2026-08-17) never mention visa sponsorship.
Of the 3,974 that explicitly rule it out, 20.2% are at employers holding
certified H-1B filings under an exactly matched name.

## Posting notes for whoever runs this

- r/dataisbeautiful requires the [OC] tag and a source/tools comment from the
  poster. Keep the comment to corpus size, date, and definitions, consistent
  with the post's methodology section.
- The 20.2% finding is the shareable one but it is also the easiest to
  misread. Lead the comments with the caveat that is already in the post: this
  is not advice to ignore a stated refusal, it is evidence that a refusal on
  one posting says little about the next posting at the same employer.
- Expect "your name matching is wrong" as the top objection. The answer is in
  the methodology section: exact-name matches only, fuzzy matches excluded
  after review found false positives, so the stated rates are conservative.
- Second likely objection: certified filings are approvals, not hires. The
  post says this. Do not overclaim in comments.
